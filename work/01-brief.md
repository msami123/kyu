## Compliance flags (HUMAN REVIEW; none blocks the brief)

1. **C2 vs. a retailer selling a third-party product.** C2 forbids third-party brands and
   trademarks; "iPhone" is Apple's trademark, yet the whole moment is Kyu selling it. Risk: an
   unlicensed brand use, plus manufacturer reseller-marketing rules that may restrict how
   resellers advertise [ASSUMPTION — not verified; Kyu would know its own terms].
   Recommended handling: a scoped exception for the Producer and a human to approve at Stage 4 —
   the word «آيفون» / "iPhone" may appear as a factual product name in on-screen text and
   voice-over only; no Apple logo, no packaging, no model name unless confirmed, no generated
   device (P2), no imitation of Apple's ad style or music. If the device must be shown, only real
   product photos supplied and cleared by Kyu, composited in the edit; otherwise it stays off
   screen, in hand from an angle with no logo, or as an unbranded silhouette. The idea should not
   depend on seeing the phone.
2. **C3 price/discount claims while the price is unknown.** Risk: any "discount", "cheapest",
   comparison or implied value ("worth the wait" was rejected for this reason) is unverifiable
   until 11/11; "he sells everything at a discount" is client-notes hearsay, not an approved claim.
   Handling: no price or value claim in any frame until the operator writes Kyu's actual
   announcement (price, models, conditions, validity) into kyu-brand.md; price lives only in the
   end-card text layer; conditions shown verbatim. "Everyone's waiting" stays a comic premise,
   never a number ("thousands are waiting"). Price-display rules for Saudi ads (e.g. how VAT or
   conditions must be shown) need a human check before any publication [ASSUMPTION — not verified].
3. **C1 — likeness of the judge himself.** Consent is user-reported, not an inspected document
   [DL-016]. Fine for the demo; before any public posting, a written release is advisable.
   No other real people; no customers.
4. **Data handling (PDPL-relevant).** kyu-brand.md still lists "Client approval to process this
   brief with both Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI (ChatGPT): TO CONFIRM", while config routes
   generator-a, writer and dop to OpenAI. DL-016 covers generation tools for his likeness, not this
   processing question. Handling: confirm before Stage 2 (Q8).
5. **C4/C5.** AI label on publication; Saudi advertising rules checked by a human before posting;
   music original or TikTok commercial library only — no trending copyrighted sound, no
   Apple-associated tracks.
