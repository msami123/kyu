## 4. Is there substantial genericization?
**Not substantially, but there is partial genericization in two directions:**
1. **"Studio" polish:** The lighting, the pressed thobe and the grey background turn an energetic comedic character into a catalogue model. The core features (moustache, eyebrows, button, pocket) remain, but the **performative energy** (talking mouth, pointing, open palm) is absent from the draft.
2. **Texture change:** The wet-look wavy hair and heavy beard shadow add a visual "fantasy" not present in the reference, and change the perceived age.

The core visual identity of the face (distribution of moustache, eyebrows and eyes, and ear structure) is well preserved.

## 5. Fixes in priority order
1. **Hair texture (06, 07, 09):** Replace the wet-look waves with a softer, thicker texture as in reference 04 (raised volume on top, slightly forward direction, softer side fade). The volume and front hairline do not change.
2. **Beard shadow (06, 08, 09):** Lighten the shadow band on the jaw, chin and cheeks to match the near-clean cheeks in 04, 05, keeping the moustache as it is. Also reduce the exaggerated pore/freckle spots.
3. **Button symbol (06–09):** Confirm its source in the original video. If it is not documented, replace it with a plain dark button or refer it to the producer to confirm it carries no third-party mark. It is not to be adopted as an anchor before that.
4. **Sandals (01, 02, 05):** Change them to simple black with wide straps as in reference 01 and simplify the buckle.
5. **Expressive energy:** Add a "talking" shot (mouth half open with raised eyebrows) and two reference hand poses (index-finger pointing, open palm) instead of settling for neutral. This is the character's signature.
6. **State of the thobe:** Introduce light folds and a less rigid drape to match the thobe's movement in 02 and 03.
7. **Profile/back angle:** Treat as extrapolation. Do not lock before review of the final product or an additional real shot.

## 6. Good anchors that must be preserved
- The thick moustache angling toward the corners of the mouth.
- The dark, thick eyebrows and their ability to rise.
- Dark brown eyes with a direct gaze.
- The volume of the hair on top and the front hairline.
- The white thobe with the short Saudi collar, the dark button, and the chest pocket on the left (relative to the wearer).
- Consistency of the pocket and button side across all angles.

- **Compliance constraint:** There is no brief and no documented consent record in the materials read for using the image/likeness of a real person (C1). A human must confirm the consent status before any production step: **HUMAN REVIEW**.
