## 3. Source-to-design comparison and improvement versus v01
**Collar and thobe detail (the user's objection):**
- In `06/07/08/09` and `01/05`: a clean rounded standing collar, and the end of the front opening is straight with no pointed tab. This addresses the objection directly. Consistency across the front, three-quarter, both profiles and the back is good: the rounded collar reads in `04-back` without an odd seam, and the pocket is on the wearer's left in 01 and 05, appears in `02-left` and is absent in `03-right` as it should be.
- **Reservation:** The collar in the face close-ups (06–09) is higher and stiffer and widens slightly toward the bottom like a funnel, whereas the source collar (03, 05, 06) looks shorter and softer. This is a proposed design, not documentation of the source, but it gives a more formal "mandarin" look than the real thobe. The seam lines on the shoulder and sleeve are still faintly visible in 06–09 and `02-left`, but less than in v01.

**Microphone:** A plain round black disc without a symbol in 01, 05 and 06–09. This resolves the C2 concern about the symbol that I raised in v01. It keeps the position at the top of the opening and is not confused with a button. In the profiles it protrudes as an oval piece at the front edge of the chest, which is reasonable. The absence of the symbol is a simplification, not a literal match to the face of the microphone in the source (references 02, 06), and this is acceptable as long as it is declared.

**Sandals:** Uniform black with crossed straps and a thick open-heel sole in 01, 02, 03, 05 and 04. The colour has been corrected from the dark blue in v01, and the buckle is gone. The source (reference 01) is a black sandal with a wide strap, so the direction is close, but the X shape is a proposed design. In `03-right` the straps look more nearly parallel than a clear X, and these are small consistency differences.

**Hair and beard:** Partial improvement. The sharpness of the side fade has been reduced, and the shadow and freckles on the cheeks have lessened versus v01. However:
- The texture on top of the hair in `06/07/09` is still wet-look waves with protruding strands, whereas source 04 has softer, thicker hair directed forward. Worst in `07` and `09`.
- The chin and jaw shadow is still visible in `08` and `09` (stronger than the near-clean cheeks in sources 04 and 05).

**Face:** The thick moustache angling toward the corners, the eyebrows, the brown eyes and the ears are preserved, and they are the strongest likeness anchors. But the face in 06 is thinner and more angular in the jaw and cheekbones, with heavier shadows under the eyes, compared with the broader and fuller face in source 04. The expressive energy has improved a lot: `08-smile` is natural, with teeth and eye creases, and `09-surprise` is closer to the character's spirit. `07`, however, is only slightly turned and is not a true three-quarter.

**State of the thobe:** Still pressed and sterile like a catalogue, without the folds of the source (references 02, 03, 06). It has not changed substantially from v01.

## 4. Anchors to preserve
The moustache with its size and angle; the eyebrows; the eyes; the volume of the hair on top and the front hairline; the rounded standing collar with the straight-ended opening (after lowering its height); the plain black microphone; the black X sandals; the chest pocket on the wearer's left; expressions 08 and 09.

## 5. Remaining issues by priority
1. **Hair texture (`06`, `07`, `09`, then 01/05):** Replace the wet-look waves with a softer, thicker texture and shorten the protruding strands, keeping the volume, direction and front hairline.
2. **Face shape (`06`):** Restore fullness to the cheeks, soften the jaw angle and lighten the under-eye shadows, to bring it closer to reference 04.
3. **Chin shadow (`08`, `09`):** Lighten it to a very light shadow while keeping the moustache.
4. **Collar height (`06–09`, and accordingly 01/05):** Lower the height by about a quarter and remove the funnel-like widening. The design stays rounded with a straight end.
5. **State of the thobe (01, 05):** Add light folds at the waist and skirt and a less rigid drape.
6. **Performance shots:** There is no "talking" shot and neither of the two hand poses (index-finger pointing, open palm) requested in v01. These are the character's signature, and they are requested in v03 or in the gesture pack.
7. **`03-right`:** Clarify the crossing of the sandal straps, and unify the character's scale between 01 and 02/03 (the front is slightly smaller on the sheet).
