# HOOK TAXONOMY

Every hook is classified on 7 dimensions. Dimension A is mandatory: ONE primary mechanism and at
most TWO secondary mechanisms. Use the codes (H01…H15) in all files.

## A. Mechanism — why does the thumb stop?
| Code | Mechanism | Example (Saudi dialect) |
|---|---|---|
| H01 | Audience call-out | "يا اللي عندك…" |
| H02 | Pain / problem | "تعبت من…؟" |
| H03 | Result first | before/after, or the ending shown first |
| H04 | Curiosity gap | missing information that makes you stay |
| H05 | Bold or contrarian claim | "كل اللي تعرفه عن… غلط" |
| H06 | Pattern interrupt | unexpected image, sound or movement |
| H07 | Direct question | "تعرف ليش…؟" |
| H08 | Number or list | "٣ أشياء…" |
| H09 | Challenge or bet | "جربت أشتري أغراض الشهر بـ ١٠٠ ريال" |
| H10 | In medias res | the story starts at its peak |
| H11 | POV | the viewer is inside the situation |
| H12 | Social proof / striking number | a long queue, a crowd of buyers |
| H13 | Shocking price or offer | especially relevant for Kyu |
| H14 | Reply to a comment | "رداً على @…" |
| H15 | Trend ride | a trending sound or format |

## B. Sensory channel (one or more)
visual | spoken | on-screen text | sound/music

## C. Face
founder | creator | employee | AI character | no face (product only)

## D. Emotion
laughter | surprise | curiosity | excitement | provocation | reassurance | nostalgia | greed (a deal not to miss)

## E. Context
sector | audience | platform | duration | organic or paid | occasion

## F. Performance (when available)
- Raw: views, 2-second and 6-second view rates, completion rate, shares, saves, comments,
  click-through (ads).
- Relative performance = the video's views ÷ the account's median views. This is the most
  important number: a million views on a huge account can be a failure; fifty thousand on a
  small account can be a hit.

## G. Verdict
why it worked | what transfers | what does not transfer
